> Here you can freely share your personal views about taking part in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This helps the FCAJ team improve, based on the aspects below.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The environment is friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to help when I run into difficulty. I would welcome more gatherings to build stronger connections.

**2. Support from Mentor / Admin Team**
The mentor gives detailed guidance, explains clearly when I am stuck, and encourages me to experiment before being handed the answer. The admin team supports documentation and creates good conditions to work effectively.

**3. Relevance to My Major**
The tasks align closely with what I studied at university, while also opening new areas such as CDN, DNS, and TLS that I had not worked with hands-on before.

**4. Learning & Skill Development**
I learned many new skills: the AWS CLI, serverless architecture, technical writing, and how to verify a deployed system methodically.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**
People respect one another, work seriously but enjoyably, and support each other. I felt like a genuine part of the community.

**6. Policies / Benefits**
The program is flexible with time and offers many chances to join internal sharing sessions.
